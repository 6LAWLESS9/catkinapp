Design a complete modern Android mobile app UI/UX for a customer complaint and rider tracking application.

The app is for customers who submit complaints or service requests. The customer does NOT assign a rider. Rider assignment is completely controlled by the admin through a separate web based admin dashboard.

The customer can submit a complaint by writing a description and optionally uploading up to 2 pictures. After submission, the complaint remains Pending until the admin manually assigns a rider.

Once the admin assigns a rider, the customer can see the assigned rider's basic information and the rider's current live location on a map.

IMPORTANT SYSTEM RULES

The customer cannot select a rider.

The customer cannot assign a rider.

The customer cannot choose a vehicle.

The customer cannot see a list of available riders.

The admin manually assigns the rider.

Before assignment the complaint status is "Pending".

After assignment the rider's current live location becomes visible to the customer.
